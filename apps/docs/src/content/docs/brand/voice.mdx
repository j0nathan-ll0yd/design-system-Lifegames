## The spine

> **The voice of a human broadcasting himself in machine-readable form — on his own terms. Legible to the machine, never surrendered to it.**
>
> A wry engineer at a terminal: precise, dry, quietly confident. It shows the work and skips the hype. It renders a life as a datastream while insisting the human is the irreducible _source_ — not an obsolete algorithm.

**Precise · Wry · Defiantly human.**

## We are / we are not

| We are                               | We are not                               |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- |
| Precise — every word earns its place | Vague, padded, or hedged                 |
| Wry — wit through precision          | Zany, jokey, breathlessly gung-ho        |
| Confident through restraint          | Boastful, hyped, self-announcing         |
| Legible to machines                  | Surrendered to them ("you are your HRV") |
| Earnest about the data               | Ironic or detached                       |
| Human-first                          | Soft, wellness-coachy, congratulatory    |
| A mirror that shows the data         | A coach that interprets your feelings    |
| Genre-literate at the right dose     | Cyberpunk cosplay                        |

## Two axes

Tone is governed on two axes in `_meta`:

- **`register`** (the voice dial): `atom` · `label` · `factual` · `expressive` · `machine` · `brand` · `consent`
- **`audience`**: `human` · `machine` · `dual`

### The arbitration rule

> Literal-first in machine surfaces; allusive only in human-narrative ones. Never sacrifice parseability for flavor in any string whose `usage[]` touches a machine surface (`llms-txt`, `llms-full`, `JSON-LD`, `.txt`, `feed`, `<SYSTEM>`). The machine gets facts; the human gets the voice.

## Principles

1. One voice, many registers.
2. Legible to machines, irreducibly human.
3. Wit through precision, not volume.
4. Show the work, skip the hype.
5. Data is a mirror, not a coach.
6. Earn the aesthetic through ethos; dose the flavor.
7. Each string does one thing.

## Do / Don't

| Context         | Don't                                                     | Do                                            |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Empty state     | "No movement yet today 💪"                                | "No movement logged yet."                     |
| Error           | "We're sorry, your session expired. Please log in again." | "Session expired. Log in to continue."        |
| Biometric       | "Your HRV looks great today!"                             | "HRV 62 ms — above 7-day baseline"            |
| Recovery        | "Your body recovers while you rest"                       | "Recovery happens at rest."                   |
| Machine surface | allusive flavor in `llms.txt`                             | factual, statistic-dense                      |
| Bio             | "Passionate, results-driven engineering leader…"          | "100% pure, old fashioned, home-grown human…" |

## Voicing biometrics

Data is a mirror, not a coach. Clinical HR-zone labels (`Bradycardia`, `Fat Burn`, `Peak Zone`) are fixed — precision is the dignity. Encouragement is implied by the data, never announced. If an honest scientist would push back on a claim as over-interpreted, don't make it.
